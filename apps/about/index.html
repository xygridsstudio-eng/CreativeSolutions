About & How to Use

What the Creative Solutions Suite is, and how to get the most out of each tool.

About

Creative Solutions is a single-entry-point suite that brings together standalone, browser-only tools behind one shared navigation sidebar. Everything runs entirely client-side — no installation, no build step, no backend, and no files ever leave your browser.

Content Check

Sentence-level comparison between a Source and Output document, with a downloadable Excel diff report.

Team Dashboard

Utilization/KPI dashboard driven by an Excel workbook, with live charts and one-click PowerPoint export.

How to Use

Content Check

  1. Upload a Source and an Output document (DOCX, PPTX, PDF, or TXT), or fetch each from a URL.
  2. Adjust Comparison Options — e.g. ignore case, extra spaces, or bullet symbols.
  3. Click Compare to see the summary and a line-by-line preview of changes.
  4. Click Download Excel Report to save the full diff report.

Team Dashboard

  1. Click Load workbook and select your Excel file (.xlsx/.xls).
  2. The KPI cards, charts, and roster table populate automatically from the workbook's data.
  3. Use the filter bar to narrow down by business unit or level.
  4. Open 🎨 Design to adjust fonts and colors, then click Build PowerPoint to export a deck.

Runs entirely in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server.
