DriveMatch Support FAQ

Q: What is the minimum booking duration for local trips?
A: The minimum local package is 3 hours at ₹300 plus ₹120 per additional hour.

Q: What is the minimum outstation fare?
A: The outstation minimum fare is ₹1,000.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: Email us at support@drivematch.example.org or call our helpline.

Q: Is overnight assistance available?
A: Yes, an overnight allowance now applies for outstation trips.
